Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Pookie Calendar ("the app") is a personal calendar and task-tracking application available at www.pookiecalendar.com. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it's used, and how it's stored.

Who runs this app

Pookie Calendar is an independently developed, personal-use application. It is not operated by a company. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to calendar@loick.org.

What information is accessed

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests:

  • Your basic Google profile information (name, email address, profile picture) to identify you as signed in.
  • Limited Google Drive access via the drive.file scope — the narrowest Drive permission Google offers. This only allows the app to read or write files that you create through the app, or that you explicitly pick using Google's file picker. The app cannot see, list, or access any other files in your Google Drive.

What data the app stores, and where

  • Your calendar tasks and theme settings are stored locally in your browser by default.
  • If you sign in with Google, your access token is also stored locally in your browser (not on any server) so you stay signed in across visits without having to sign in again each time. This token only grants the limited drive.file access described above and expires on its own after about an hour if not renewed.
  • If you choose to use the optional "Save to Drive" feature, your tasks and theme settings are saved as plain JSON files in your own Google Drive, in files created by and only accessible to this app (via the drive.file scope). These files remain your property — you can view, download, or delete them at any time from your Google Drive.
  • The app does not have its own server or database. Nothing you enter is sent to or stored by the developer — it stays either in your browser or in your own Google Drive.

How data is used

Your data is used solely to provide the app's functionality: displaying your calendar, saving and loading your tasks and theme, and letting you sign in with Google. The app does not sell, rent, or share your personal information or Google account data with any third party. The app does not use your data for advertising and does not run third-party analytics or tracking scripts.

Data retention and deletion

Since your data lives in your own browser and your own Google Drive, you are in full control of it:

  • Clearing your browser's local storage removes locally saved tasks, theme settings, and your stored sign-in token.
  • Deleting the app's files from your Google Drive removes anything saved there.
  • Using the "Sign out" button in the app immediately deletes your locally stored access token and revokes it with Google.
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account permissions page.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to calendar@loick.org.
